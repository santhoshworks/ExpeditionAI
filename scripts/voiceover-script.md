# ThoughtMap Demo Video - Voiceover Script

> **Total Duration:** ~2:30 - 3:00 minutes
> **Tone:** Friendly, confident, conversational. Think "smart friend showing you something cool."
> **Voice:** ElevenLabs - Recommended voices: "Josh" (warm male), "Rachel" (clear female), or "Adam" (professional male)
> **Pace:** Moderate. Pauses between scenes to let visuals breathe.

---

## Scene 1: Landing Page (~12s)

**[Screen: Landing page hero section, scrolling through features]**

> "Ever feel like learning something new turns into a mess of scattered browser tabs and half-watched YouTube videos? What if there was a better way to explore any subject — one that actually keeps everything organized?"

**Pause: 2s** (scroll back to top)

---

## Scene 2: Login (~8s)

**[Screen: Typing credentials and logging in]**

> "Let me show you ThoughtMap — an AI-powered learning platform where you chat, explore, and truly understand any topic."

---

## Scene 3: Dashboard Overview (~12s)

**[Screen: Dashboard with analytics cards and expedition list]**

> "This is your dashboard. It shows all your learning expeditions at a glance — how many trails you've explored, topics you've covered, and your overall progress. Think of it as mission control for your learning."

---

## Scene 4: Create/Open Expedition (~10s)

**[Screen: Opening or creating an expedition on "Machine Learning Fundamentals"]**

> "Let's dive into an expedition. Each one starts with a topic you want to learn. Today, we'll explore machine learning fundamentals."

---

## Scene 5: Chat Interface (~18s)

**[Screen: Typing a message, AI response streaming in, scrolling through the answer]**

> "Here's where the magic happens. Just ask a question — like 'explain the key concepts of neural networks' — and the AI gives you a clear, structured explanation. But here's what makes ThoughtMap different: as you learn, you can branch into new trails for any concept that sparks your curiosity, without ever losing track of where you started."

---

## Scene 6: Teaching Mode Toggle (~6s)

**[Screen: Switching between Coach and Content modes]**

> "You can switch between coaching mode, where the AI guides you with questions, and content mode for straight explanations. Your choice."

---

## Scene 7: Flashcard Creation (~12s)

**[Screen: Pasting content, generating flashcards]**

> "Ready to lock in what you've learned? Paste any content and ThoughtMap generates smart flashcards automatically. It pulls out the key concepts so you don't have to."

---

## Scene 8: Spaced Repetition Review (~10s)

**[Screen: Flipping flashcards, rating them]**

> "Review your cards with spaced repetition — the same technique used by medical students and language learners. Rate how well you know each concept, and the system schedules your next review at the perfect time."

---

## Scene 9: Wishlist (~6s)

**[Screen: Viewing the learning wishlist]**

> "Found something interesting during a conversation? Save it to your wishlist so you never forget what you want to explore next."

---

## Scene 10: Settings & Dark Mode (~6s)

**[Screen: Toggling dark mode, seeing the app in dark theme]**

> "And yes — dark mode. Because late-night learning sessions deserve easy-on-the-eyes styling."

---

## Scene 11: Closing Shot (~12s)

**[Screen: Dashboard final scroll]**

> "ThoughtMap turns chaotic curiosity into structured understanding. Eight curated AI models. Visual learning maps. Personalized coaching. And everything you need to finally master the topics that matter to you. Start your free expedition today."
