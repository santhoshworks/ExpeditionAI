Study Schedule Planning Guide
1

Assess Your Commitments

Before filling in study blocks, mark all your fixed commitments:

  • Classes and lectures
  • Work hours
  • Extracurricular activities
  • Meals and sleep (non-negotiable!)
  • Commute time
2

Identify Your Peak Hours

Schedule difficult subjects during your peak mental energy:

  • Morning person? Math/science early
  • Night owl? Deep work in evening
  • Save routine tasks for low-energy times
  • Never schedule hard topics after meals
3

Apply the 2-Hour Rule

For every hour of class, plan 2 hours of study:

  • 1 hour for review and homework
  • 1 hour for deeper learning
  • Adjust based on course difficulty
  • Include buffer time before exams
4

Build in Flexibility

Life happens. Build a schedule that can adapt:

  • Leave 20% of time unscheduled
  • Have backup study times identified
  • Plan catch-up sessions on weekends
  • Review and adjust weekly
