How to Use Your Cornell Notes Template

Master the proven method used by top students worldwide

1

During the Lecture or Reading

Use the Notes column (right side) to capture key information. Don't try to write everything - focus on main ideas, examples, definitions, and concepts. Use abbreviations and your own shorthand. Leave the Cue column blank for now so you can focus fully on understanding.

2

Within 24 Hours After

Review your notes while the information is fresh. Fill in the Cue column with questions, keywords, and prompts. Use our AI Learning Prompts to generate meaningful questions that test understanding. This 10-15 minute review session dramatically improves retention.

3

Write Your Summary

Complete the Summary section at the bottom in your own words. Aim for 2-3 sentences that capture the essence. If you struggle to summarize, that's a signal you need to review the material more deeply. This synthesis is crucial for long-term memory.

4

Review with Active Recall

During study sessions, cover the Notes column and use only the Cues to test yourself. This active recall practice is one of the most effective learning techniques. Check the Review Schedule boxes as you complete each review session.

For Digital Use
This template works great with tablet apps like Notability, GoodNotes, or OneNote. Import the PDF and write directly on it with your stylus.
For Print Use
Print multiple copies and keep them in a binder organized by subject. The consistent format makes review sessions more efficient.
Active Recall Tips
When testing yourself, speak your answers out loud or write them on a separate sheet. Don't just think "I know this" - prove it!
Spaced Repetition
Review at increasing intervals: 24 hours, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month. Each review strengthens the memory trace.
