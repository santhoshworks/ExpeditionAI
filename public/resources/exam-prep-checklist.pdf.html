During the Exam: Proven Strategies

1. Read Everything First
Spend 5 minutes reading all questions before answering. Note point values and identify easy wins. Plan your time allocation based on difficulty and points.
2. Answer Easy Questions First
Build confidence and secure points. Mark difficult questions and return to them. Don't waste time stuck on one problem while easy points wait.
3. Show Your Work
Partial credit adds up. Write out your reasoning even if unsure. For essays, outline first. Clear structure impresses graders.
4. Time Management
Allocate time per question based on points. Set checkpoints (e.g., halfway at 50% time). Save 10% of time for review at the end.

Question Type Strategies

Multiple Choice
Eliminate obviously wrong answers first. Watch for absolute words (always, never) - often wrong. "All of the above" is often correct. Trust your first instinct unless you find clear evidence otherwise.
Short Answer
Be concise but complete. Start with your main point. Use keywords from the question. If unsure, write what you know - something is better than nothing.
Essay Questions
Outline for 2-3 minutes first. Clear thesis statement. One idea per paragraph. Use specific examples. Strong conclusion that restates key points.
Problem Solving
Write down given information first. Identify what you're solving for. Show each step clearly. Check units. Verify answer makes sense in context.
